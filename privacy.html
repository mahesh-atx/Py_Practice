Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 3, 2026 · Effective immediately for PyPractice

1. Overview

PyPractice ("we", "our", or "the platform") is an open interactive learning platform accessible at https://mahesh-atx.github.io/Py_Practice/. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any information you share while using our platform.

2. Information We Collect

  • Google Account Information: When you sign in with Google, we receive basic public profile information (your full name, email address, and profile picture URL) through Google OAuth. We never request access to your contacts, private files, or drive.
  • Practice & Code Progress: Which problems you have solved, your streak statistics, and code snippets you draft in the editor.

3. How Your Data Is Stored & Used

Your data is stored securely in Google Cloud Firestore under strict per-user security rules (`users/{userId}`), and cached locally on your device in browser `localStorage`. We use this data exclusively to:

  • Save your problem solutions and track your Python learning achievements across multiple devices.
  • Display your profile details on the user profile page.
  • We never sell, rent, monetize, or share your personal data with third-party advertisers.

4. User Rights & Data Control

You maintain full ownership of your data. You can:

  • Export your progress: Download all your solved challenges and activity in JSON format from the Profile page at any time.
  • Reset or delete data: Click "Reset Progress" on the Profile or Topics page to wipe all stored progress.
  • Disconnect Google Access: Revoke PyPractice permissions at any time via your Google Account Permissions.

If you have any questions or privacy concerns regarding PyPractice, contact us at: dongaremahesh10@gmail.com.
